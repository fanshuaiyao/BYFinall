   An essential aspect of drug design is the ability to specify the
target properties in our generative process. This section describes our
implementation of the guidance scheme for our diffusion model, which
we refer to as the conditional diffusion model. We first describe the
creation of a regressor that predicts properties based on graph structure,
and then formulate the conditional process that guides the diffusion
model to push it to generate molecules with the desired properties.
